Eligibility

MBBS Eligibility Criteria

Understand the requirements before applying for MBBS admission in India.

NEET Qualification

  • Valid NEET UG score
  • Minimum qualifying percentile
  • Score valid for current admission year

Academic Criteria

  • 50% in PCB (General)
  • 40% in PCB (Reserved)
  • Physics, Chemistry & Biology compulsory

Indian Student Eligibility

  • Indian citizenship
  • 17+ years of age
  • Valid NEET score

NRI Student Eligibility

  • NRI/OCI/PIO status
  • Valid passport & visa
  • Valid NEET score

Required Documents

  • NEET Scorecard
  • 10th & 12th Marksheets
  • Category & Domicile Certificates

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MBBS admissions in India.

The minimum NEET qualifying percentile varies by category. General category requires 50th percentile, OBC/SC/ST requires 40th, and General-PwBD requires 45th percentile. Cutoffs also vary by college and quota.
Yes, NRI students can apply under the NRI quota in many medical colleges. They need a valid NEET score, proof of NRI status, passport, visa, and sponsor documents. NRI quota offers separate reserved seats.
Government quota seats are allocated through centralized state counseling based on NEET merit and are more affordable. Management quota seats are in private colleges, filled directly by the college, and have higher fees but more flexible admission criteria.
Yes, NEET UG is mandatory for all MBBS and BDS admissions in India, regardless of quota or category. Without a valid NEET score, admission to MBBS is not possible.
Typically required: NEET scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets, birth certificate, identity proof, and category certificate. For NRI, additional documents like passport, visa, NRI/OCI certificate, and sponsor documents are needed.
